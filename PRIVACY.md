# Privacy Policy — NaviGo Fusion 360 Add-in

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026  
**Developer:** Eslam Eldeknawy (Eslam's Lab)

## Data Collection
The NaviGo Autodesk Fusion 360 Add-in operates 100% locally on your computer. It communicates exclusively with the NaviGo hardware controller over a local USB serial (COM) port and interacts with the local Autodesk Fusion 360 Camera API.

## No Telemetry or Tracking
* We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, CAD models, design files, usage analytics, or telemetry data.
* All user preferences (speed sliders, axis inversions, and lighting themes) are stored locally on your machine.
